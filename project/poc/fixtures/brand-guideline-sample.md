# Nova Athletics Brand Guideline Sample

Nova Athletics helps everyday runners feel ready for the next mile. The brand voice is energetic, clear, and performance-led without sounding elitist.

## Tone

- Energetic
- Clear
- Performance-led
- Optimistic

## Copy Rules

- Keep headlines concise and active.
- Favor durable performance language over hype.
- CTA copy should stay short enough for mobile placements.
- Avoid claims that imply medical, guaranteed, or miraculous performance outcomes.

## Banned Phrases

- cheap
- miracle

## Locale Notes

Preserve concise CTA style across locales. Do not translate idioms literally when they would sound forced in the target market.
